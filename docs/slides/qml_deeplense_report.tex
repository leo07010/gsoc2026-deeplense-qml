\begin{frame}{Task, data, and the regime that matters}
\textbf{Task.} Classify $64\times64$ strong-lensing images:
\qq{axion} (vortex) / \textbf{CDM} (subhalos) / \textbf{no\_sub} (smooth).
3-class macro-OVR AUC, 9:1 split, best-val-AUC checkpoint, seed-paired multi-seed runs.
\smallskip

\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\normalsize
\smallskip

\begin{block}{Why low-$N$}
LSST / Euclid / Roman will deliver many \emph{unlabeled} images and few
\emph{labeled} ones. We sweep down to $N{=}100$ labels/class --- exactly where the
pretraining-based classical SOTA is weakest.
\end{block}

\footnotesize
\textbf{Reference target.} MAE-pretrained ViT, AUC $0.968$ (arXiv:2512.06642);
our protocol-matched reproduction reaches $0.9895$ (Model\_II, full data).
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Module-level: the quantum contribution is statistically real}
\scriptsize
\textbf{Stage 1} ($n{=}10$, Holm over 6 tests) flagged one cell as marginal:
Model\_II $N{=}250$, Q$>$sham, $8/10$, $p_{\mathrm{raw}}{=}0.0098$,
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}{=}0.059$. \;
\textbf{Stage 2} was then pre-registered as a sequential confirmation with
$n{=}20$ and $\alpha{=}0.01$ \emph{fixed before launch}:
\smallskip

\begin{tabular}{@{}llcccl@{}}
\toprule
Cell & contrast & pos & mean $\Delta$ [95\% CI] & $p$ & verdict \\
\midrule
Model\_II $N{=}250$ & Q$>$sham  & 15/20 & $+0.0140$ $[+0.0048,+0.0233]$ &
  \gd{\textbf{0.0032}} & \gd{\textbf{significant}} \\
Model\_II $N{=}250$ & Q$>$plain & 11/20 & $+0.0081$ $[-0.0010,+0.0172]$ &
  0.088 & \bd{n.s.} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\normalsize
\smallskip

\footnotesize
\begin{block}{We located where the effect lives}
Swapping the entangling circuit for rotations only (NOENT) changes nothing in the
residual architecture: $\Delta_{\mathrm{ENT}-\mathrm{NOENT}}={+}0.0022$ ($p{=}0.22$) at
$N{=}250$ and $-0.0000$ ($p{=}0.47$) at $N{=}500$, while both stay above SHAM.
$\Rightarrow$ the contribution stems from the \qq{\textbf{amplitude-embedding
structure}} (a normalized $256$-dim state built by the Neural Amplitude Encoder)
and the multi-basis readout, \textbf{not} from the entangling ring.
\end{block}

\textbf{Honest size.} $+1.4\%$ AUC is statistically significant but sits
\emph{below} our $3\%$ practical bar, and it is a single cell.
\end{frame}

% --------------------------------------------------- 9
\begin{frame}{What we do \emph{not} claim}
\begin{itemize}
\item \alert{\textbf{No quantum advantage.}} The significant module-level effect is
      $+1.4\%$ AUC --- \textbf{below our own $3\%$ practical bar}, in a single
      $(N{=}250)$ cell. Too small to justify quantum hardware on this task.
\item \textbf{No model-level win.} vs.\ the plain CNN the contrast is
      \bd{not significant} ($p{=}0.088$, $11/20$). The plain-CNN floor is
      \textbf{guaranteed by construction, not won empirically} ---
      we report it as a floor only.
\item \textbf{The paradigm win is not the circuit's.} It belongs to the CNN
      inductive-bias family; the plain CNN beats MAE-ft too.
\item \textbf{Simulation only.} All quantum results use exact state-vector
      simulation (PennyLane \texttt{default.qubit}, backprop) --- no NISQ-hardware
      runs, no noise model, no shot-noise study.
\end{itemize}
\smallskip

\footnotesize
A theoretical bound matches the small size: since $\mathbf{m}=g(\mathbf{h})$ is
deterministic, the data-processing inequality gives
$I(Y;(\mathbf{h},\mathbf{m}))=I(Y;\mathbf{h})$ --- the branch reshapes decision
geometry, it cannot add label information. Negative levers (wide readout, qubit
scaling, TDA, QMAE) are documented in full in the paper.
\end{frame}

\begin{frame}{Conclusion --- the three-tier claim ladder}
\footnotesize
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Paradigm} (vs.\ MAE-ViT): \gd{\textbf{significant}} ---
      Holm $p{=}0.003$, $10/10$ seed-paired, $+22$--$28$\,pp; $10/11$ cells over the
      pre-registered bar across three datasets; $1/19$ params and \emph{zero}
      pretraining data. Credit to the \textbf{CNN family}.
\item \textbf{Module} (circuit vs.\ matched sham): \gd{\textbf{statistically
      significant}} at (II,\,250) under a pre-registered two-stage design
      ($n{=}20$, $p{=}0.0032<\alpha{=}0.01$); magnitude $+1.4\%$,
      \alert{below our $3\%$ practical bar}; driven by amplitude-embedding
      structure, \textbf{not} entanglement.
\item \textbf{Model} (vs.\ plain CNN): \gd{floor guaranteed by construction};
      vs.-plain \bd{not significant} ($p{=}0.088$) $\Rightarrow$ floor only,
      no model-level claim.
\end{enumerate}
\normalsize
\smallskip

\begin{alertblock}{The honest headline}
\textbf{We do not claim quantum advantage.} We claim a significance-tested
\emph{module-level} contribution, localized to amplitude-embedding geometry, plus a
large, reproducible \emph{paradigm-level} win for a tiny non-pretrained hybrid in the
low-label regime that upcoming surveys will actually face.
\end{alertblock}
